Website audit

Harris has the experience. The website just isn’t showing it yet.

Harris has 30+ years on big construction projects, a long list of certifications, and partners like Swinerton, Cahill and Build Group. But when a contractor or city agency visits the website today, they see one page with no current projects and very little detail. Here’s what we found and how to fix it.

What we found on harrishoisting.com · October 2026
AreaWhat we foundHow to fix it
Current projects
Your current work isn’t on the site, so visitors can’t see what you’re working on now.
Add a Projects section with photos and a few lines on each job, using only the wording each client approves.
Company one-pager
There’s no one-page company summary to download. Contractors and city agencies usually ask for this first when choosing who to hire.
Create a one-page PDF (called a capability statement) with your certifications, license number, services and contact info, plus a download button on the site.
Certifications
Your certifications are listed in two places on the site, and the two lists don’t match.
Confirm the correct list with you, then use that same list everywhere.
Services
Your 7 services are listed one line each, with no explanation and no easy way to ask for a crew.
Give each service a short description and a “Request a crew” button.
Client quotes & partners
There’s only one client quote, and some partner logos show up more than once.
Add more quotes from happy clients and clean up the partner logos.
Showing up on Google
When the site appears on Google, its title just says “HOME.” Links shared by text or on social media show no preview picture.
Give each page a clear title and short description, like “Harris Hoisting · Union hoist and crane operators in San Francisco,” and add a preview picture.
Phones & accessibility
On a phone, visitors can’t pinch to zoom in. Most images have no written description, which people using screen readers rely on and Google reads too.
Turn zoom back on and add a short description to every image.
Team & hiring
There’s nothing about Tana, the team, or how to come work for Harris.
Add an About section with Tana’s story, and a Careers page for union operators.
Social media links
The “Connect” section at the bottom of the page is empty.
Link your LinkedIn (and Instagram, once it’s live) so visitors can follow along.
If you fix only three things, fix these

1. Show what you’re working on now. Current projects are the strongest proof Harris is busy and trusted. We only post what each client allows, and Tana signs off first.

2. Add the company one-pager. It makes it easy for contractors and agencies to choose Harris and pass your info along.

3. Use one correct certification list. Certifications help win work, so they need to match everywhere: the website, Procore and agency vendor lists.
