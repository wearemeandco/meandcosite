Where your donation goes

We're raising $2,000 for two causes close to our hearts: women entrepreneurs, and childcare for working parents. ALL money raised is donated to these two organizations.

Friends of Puerto Rico · Metro Women's Business Center

Friends of Puerto Rico is a nonprofit that invests in the island's people and economy. Its Metro Women's Business Center helps women entrepreneurs in the San Juan metro area start and grow their businesses, with training, one-on-one advising and access to capital.

Visit their site
Criar en Clave

Criar en Clave is a coworking space with childcare, in nature, in Humacao. Parents work while their kids (ages 3 to 9) play, paint, cook and learn, with scholarships for families in transition. It was started by Christine E. Nieves and Luis Rodríguez Sánchez, who organized their neighbors in barrio Mariana after Hurricane María. Your gift helps open the space and keep it running its first year.

Visit their site
Why we keep coming back

After Hurricane María, Melody raised $15,000 and spent about a month in Puerto Rico fixing roofs and helping communities rebuild. In 2019 she went back to Mariana with two friends to clean the community center and help the women cooking for the neighborhood. In 2022 that became Coquito Conmigo, and it's become our holiday tradition of giving back to the island.
