10 Founder-Friendly Social Post Prompts for When Your Brain Is 😴

December 18, 2025

December is a strange season for small businesses. You’re reflecting, closing loops, thinking about next year — and still expected to post consistently.

This is a simple, low-pressure read to help you get ahead as we head into 2026. Instead of forcing originality, these prompts are meant to meet you where you are.

  1. One lesson this year changed how you work. Not a highlight reel — a real shift in thinking.
  2. A behind-the-scenes moment from your week. Messy desk, coffee runs, notes app chaos. Humans connect to process.
  3. A question your clients or community ask you all the time. Answer it once. Save yourself 12 DMs later.
  4. Something you tried that didn’t work — and what you learned. This builds trust faster than wins alone.
  5. A “before vs. now” perspective. Your mindset, systems, boundaries, or confidence — growth isn’t always revenue.
  6. A recommendation you genuinely stand by. Tool, book, habit, or resource you actually use.
  7. What you’re focusing on less going into the new year. Boundaries are content too.
  8. A client or collaborator appreciation post. Specific praise > generic shoutouts.
  9. One belief you no longer agree with in your industry. This sparks conversation without being polarizing.
  10. A simple reminder your audience probably needs right now. If you’re thinking it, someone else needs to hear it.

You don’t need viral ideas. You need clear, honest ones. Pick one, press publish, and move on with your day.

I’ll be honest — I needed to get content out myself (yes, even as a marketing studio), but I’ve been heads-down on other pressing priorities. So for the end of the year, I kept it simple: just three posts.

As I close out the year, my content focus is intentional and grounded in the following:

Sharing 2025 wins — taking time to reflect on what worked, what landed, and the progress made. Reflection keeps us motivated and reminds us why the work matters.

Clarifying focus and vision for the year ahead — sharing where I’m headed next and how I can continue to create value for the people and brands I support.

Ending the year with gratitude and momentum — a genuine New Year message that sets the tone for what’s to come.
