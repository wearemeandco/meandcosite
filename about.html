About Me&Co.

Marketing with strategy and heart.

Me&Co. exists so founders don't have to choose between building their business and being seen. We bring the structure, you bring your marketing needs.

The Founder Story

Me&Co. was built from a simple belief: founders deserve a marketing partner who understands the business behind the brand.

After years leading marketing and operations inside startups, universities, and founder programs, Melody Estrada saw the same challenge everywhere. Founders weren't looking for more content. They needed someone who could connect strategy, systems, and storytelling in a way that actually moved their business forward.

What began through necessity and referrals from founders who trusted her work has grown into a culturally rooted marketing studio helping businesses build visibility with intention. Today, Me&Co. partners with founders, startups, and entrepreneur-support organizations through brand strategy, content, websites, marketing systems, and hands-on education—from one-on-one advisory to workshops inside accelerators and university programs.

At its core, Me&Co. believes marketing should create opportunity, not just attention. That's why you'll also find us bringing founders together through community events, supporting initiatives that strengthen small businesses, and sharing practical insights through resources written for real entrepreneurs—not algorithms.
