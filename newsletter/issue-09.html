Hispanic Heritage Month, two new gatherings, and money on the table

4 min read

Issue 4 · October 2026

¡Feliz día, mi gente! We’re coming to you halfway through Hispanic Heritage Month with some exciting things we’ve been cooking up. We have two gatherings on the calendar, both fun to attend and both with heart: Coquito Conmigo, our annual virtual coquito session raising money for Puerto Rico, and Capital & Craft, a brand-new workshop series with our good friends at Arauz Inc., the accounting firm in Cutler Bay, FL. And as always, we’ve got the grants, a roundup of Hispanic Heritage Month events, and resources to support your health and your finances. Let’s jump in!

What We’re Building
In person · Cutler Bay, FL
Capital & Craft

A workshop series for founders and those building alongside them, focused on establishing a strong financial and operational foundation for your business. In partnership with Arauz Inc.

Three Wednesdays, 6:30 to 8:30 PM ET
•  January 27, 2027
•  February 10, 2027
•  February 24, 2027
$80 for all three sessions. Ten to twelve seats, so registration is required.
Exclusive attendee benefits
Attend all three sessions and unlock exclusive benefits designed to strengthen your business’s foundation:
•  $100 off your first business tax return prepared by Arauz Inc.
•  A complimentary Financial Checkup + Action Plan from Arauz Inc.: a personalized review of your bookkeeping setup, business/entity structure, chart of accounts, payroll setup and basic tax considerations, followed by a customized plan to keep your business organized, prepared and tax ready.
•  A complimentary Brand and Marketing Checkup from Me&Co.: a 20-minute virtual review of your positioning, messaging, website, social presence and customer journey, with a personalized marketing action plan of 3 to 5 prioritized recommendations.
Benefits must be redeemed within 60 days of the event.

Coquito Conmigo

Since 2018, Coquito Conmigo has been our holiday tradition of raising money for Puerto Rico. The island is near and dear to my heart, especially after all the work I’ve done there, and getting together over coquito every year is one of my favorite ways to give back.

Register to receive the ingredient list, and stay for a raffle for a free marketing consultation. Minimum $5 donation, with all proceeds going to Puerto Rico.
