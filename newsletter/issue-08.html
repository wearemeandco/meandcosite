What We've Been Up To

We went to a FICPA (Florida Institute of CPAs) networking event in South Miami, and it turned out to be one of the more organic, grounding rooms we've been in this year. Nothing fancy, just real connection, something that feels increasingly rare these days. We wrote affirmations for ourselves, and I took it a step further and wrote one for a friend too. A small thing, but it's the kind of encouragement that carries you through the daily grind of founder life. The women there were business owners, but also moms, wives, and people with hobbies and full lives outside of work. It was a good reminder of how nice it is to be in community outside of AI and screens for a minute. What's an event you've been to lately that left you feeling more human and connected?
