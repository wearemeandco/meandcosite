What We’ve Been Up To
Oct 21 · Miami
Positioned. Paid. Published.

On October 21, I joined hosts Maha Abouelenein and Erika Cramer in Miami for Positioned. Paid. Published., a full day for women who have already built something real and are ready to be known for it. We worked through our positioning, our reputation, what makes our perspective different, and the rooms we need to be in.

My biggest takeaways

I had just come back from three weeks away visiting family, and I did not need to be the loudest voice in the room. I needed to sit, absorb, and remember principles I was raised on, at home, in school, and in my corporate career. It filled my energy tank. Three things stuck with me:

•  Be intentional with everything. Dress, think, feel, and act like the person you want to be. Erika spoke about confidence, and how the things you tell yourself on repeat are what get you into the right rooms.
•  Network with strategy, not volume. Networking is more than handing out business cards or adding people on LinkedIn. Watch and listen to who is speaking, spot the strategic connections, and align yourself with people you can help and people who can help move you forward. Maha Abouelenein shared how she prepares: she does her homework on the event, who the speakers are, and who she needs to get in front of, and she definitely makes it happen!
•  Follow up and follow through. They are two different things. Following up is touching base after a first conversation. Following through is actually doing what you said you would do. Both matter, and both speak volumes to your character and how you do business.
Tue, Oct 27 · Wynwood, Miami
Using AI to Build Systems That Build Your Business

On October 27, I had the honor of being the guest speaker at a SkyeCo meeting at Mindspace in Wynwood. It was my first time speaking in front of a room in two years, so I will admit I was a little nervous.

I spoke about using AI to build systems that build your business, and we had a great conversation about which tools are actually working for the people in the room. My main takeaway for the group was this: don’t gather more AI tools. Instead, find out what the repetitive work in your business really is, and then build systems around it.

SkyeCo was founded by Lisa Skye, and you can feel how intentional she is about the space she has built. Lisa was the second employee at WeWork, where she spent 14 months as its founding community manager, and she went on to co-found Primary, an eco-friendly coworking space. She is a true community builder, and SkyeCo is her latest venture: intentional business networking for seasoned professionals in growth mode, built on the belief that generosity fuels opportunity.

I walked away with new friends and a few possible opportunities, and I am looking forward to seeing what comes of joining them. Visit SkyeCo’s website to learn more.
