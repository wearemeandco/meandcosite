Coquito for a cause: this Saturday, and how you can help Puerto Rico

3 min read

Issue 6

¡Hola, mi gente! This one is all about coquito. Coquito Conmigo is this Saturday, and it is so much more than a morning of making coquito together. It is how we raise money for nonprofits in Puerto Rico, and this year we are supporting two causes that are close to my heart: women entrepreneurs, and childcare support for working parents through coworking spaces. As always, you will also find grants open this month and the tools we use. Let’s jump in!

What We’ve Been Up To
Thu, Nov 12 · Coquito prep
The Coquito Shopping Is Done

Today we did our shopping for Saturday. Everything is ready, and now all that is missing is you. Grab your blender and come make coquito with us.

What We’re Building
Sat, Nov 14 · 10 to 11:30 AM ET · Zoom
Coquito Conmigo

Since 2022, Coquito Conmigo has been our holiday tradition: we make coquito together and raise money for Puerto Rico. Register to receive the ingredient list, then join us on Zoom Saturday morning. A $5 minimum donation holds your spot.

Where your donation goes
•  Metro Women’s Business Center, with Friends of Puerto Rico, supporting women entrepreneurs on the island. Learn more
•  Criar Enclave, childcare support for working parents through coworking spaces. Learn more

Our goal is to raise $2,000 by Giving Tuesday, December 1. We would be so grateful for anyone who donates, in any amount.

A raffle, too. We will be raffling off a free 30-minute marketing consulting session with me.

In Miami? We are also selling coquito, and every bottle sold goes toward our goal. To get a bottle, email us directly (just reply to this email).
