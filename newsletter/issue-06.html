Hola Me&Co. Fam! It has been a minute, and we have been heads down. There is a lot to catch you up on, so let's get into it.

First, the big one: we have a new look. This refresh reflects the bolder direction we are taking. The colors come straight from my Caribbean roots, and they stand for what we are building toward: cultura, bolder moves, and systems that actually carry our founders.

So what is new? Our look, our colors, our website, a new collab with Mora Studios, and our services. We are moving past services into systems that help founders work faster and smarter. As we grow and evolve with our clients, we are always looking for ways to help them tell their story and work efficiently with AI tools.
