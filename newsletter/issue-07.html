What We've Been Up To

We attended "Navigating Faith, Family, Business & Life with Excellence" this month, a session on building a personal mission statement from a Christ-focused perspective. The idea: your mission statement should be a lens for every business and life decision, not a comfort blanket. The Bible, as one speaker put it, "is not a journal of comfort, it's about sanctification through waiting and suffering."

A few of the questions they posed to ground that mission:

•  What has God put on your heart?
•  What is your role?
•  How do you want to be remembered?
•  What talents have you been given?
•  What are you ultimately living for?

The gut-check question underneath all of them: am I in alignment with how I am living?

What Our Friends & Family Are Doing

Friend of the newsletter alert! Yvette Ramirez of Kuro Libros just opened pre-orders for her first bilingual children's book, Mi Familia Cubana con Kuro. Kuro Libros is dedicated to empowering parents and kids to engage with Latin American and Caribbean culture and language, building confidence and resilience through language-learning and a strong sense of connection and community. Using Kuro the cat as a guide, the books help young readers understand who they are, where they came from, and how they contribute to the world, all with joy, humor, and openness. Pre-order now and follow @KuroLibros on IG/TikTok for updates.
